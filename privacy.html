Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

1. Information We Process

The Telegram AI Assistant processes messages, questions, photos, voice messages, and files submitted by users. It may also retrieve information from the internet to answer questions.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to answer questions, process images, retrieve information, and provide productivity features.

3. Google Gemini API

Messages, photos, and other supported content may be sent to Google's Gemini API for AI processing and response generation.

4. Google Account Integration

Users may optionally connect Gmail and Google Calendar through Google's authentication system. Authorized information is used to provide requested Gmail and Calendar functionality.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising purposes. Information is transmitted to necessary service providers, including Telegram for messaging and Google for AI processing and authorized integrations.

6. Data Storage and Security

Information handling and retention depend on the services used to operate the assistant. We aim to protect user information and limit access to authorized services.

7. User Privacy Rights

Users may stop using the assistant at any time and revoke Google permissions through their Google Account security settings. Privacy questions can be directed to the developer through the associated GitHub repository.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the assistant's features and data practices change.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Telegram AI Assistant's GitHub repository.
